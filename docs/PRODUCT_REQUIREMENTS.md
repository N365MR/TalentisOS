# Product Requirements

## Purpose

TalentisOS is the leadership playbook a new leader opens to run the workday. It helps answer what matters today, what carried over, what needs a decision, what is at risk, what needs discussion, what requires follow-up, what must be completed, what should be prepared for tomorrow, and what should improve.

## Core flow

End of Day → Morning Huddle → Today’s Work.

## Leadership rhythm

Prepare → Align → Execute → Review → Improve.

## Primary navigation

- **Today:** the concise operating view of the current day.
- **Huddle:** prepare alignment around priorities, carry-over, risks, and decisions.
- **Work:** hold focused work items and follow-up commitments.
- **Review:** close the day, identify unfinished work, and prepare tomorrow.
- **Improve:** capture small, practical improvements to the operating rhythm.

Every screen has one primary action. Secondary actions must not compete visually or conceptually with the main next step.

## Phase 2 requirements

New users complete a five-step, one-question-at-a-time setup covering function, three outcomes, workday start, morning huddle, and review time. Progress saves after each step and can move backward. The completion screen says “TalentisOS is ready.” and offers “Start Today”. Returning users open directly to Today.

Today surfaces greeting/date, daily focus, up to three priorities, carryover, risks, decisions, follow-ups, meetings, a local leadership prompt, and end-of-day status. Focus statements use deterministic local rules; no external AI is used.

## Phase 4 requirements

Work is a focused daily leadership operating view, not a project-management board. It groups work into Now, Next, Later, and Waiting. Supported types are Priority, Risk, Decision, Follow-up, and Action. Now contains no more than three active leadership actions. Work items may carry optional plain-text responsibility and related IDs, but no person or employee entity exists.

## Phase 5 requirements

Review reuses existing day records to guide completion, open items, risks, carryover, escalation, improvement, and likely tomorrow priorities. Finish Day saves a complete snapshot, confirms a small tomorrow plan, and makes the day available in read-only history with an explicit Edit Day action.

## Boundaries

No employee profile entity, employee personal information, HR records, performance reviews, payroll, recruitment, skills matrices, surveillance, time tracking, CRM, ERP, full project management, chat, email, or complex workflow builders.
