# TalentisOS User Manual

TalentisOS is a local-first leadership operating system for new and emerging leaders. It helps you prepare the day, align people around the work, keep commitments moving, review what happened, and make small improvements.

Live app: <https://n365mr.github.io/TalentisOS/>

## Important: where your data lives

TalentisOS stores your workspace in the browser on your current device. Your records are not sent to a TalentisOS account, cloud database, analytics service, or external tracker.

Your data remains on the device unless you deliberately export it. Export a JSON backup before clearing browser data, changing devices, or uninstalling the app.

## The leadership rhythm

TalentisOS is organised around five simple movements:

1. **Prepare** — decide what matters and notice risk before the day becomes noisy.
2. **Align** — make priorities, ownership, decisions, and blockers visible.
3. **Execute** — keep a small amount of focused work moving.
4. **Review** — close the day, capture learning, and prepare tomorrow.
5. **Improve** — turn a practical observation into one small change to test.

The suggested rhythm is:

**End of Day → Morning Huddle → Today’s Work → End of Day**

## Quick start

### First launch

1. Open the live URL or launch the installed app.
2. On the welcome screen, select **Build My First 90 Days**.
3. Answer the five setup questions:
   - Your leadership situation
   - The type of work your team does
   - How much structure you prefer in guidance
   - Your normal workday start time
   - Your suggested end-of-day review time
4. Use **Back** to change an answer.
5. Use **Save & resume later** if you need to leave setup.
6. Select **Finish setup**.
7. On the completion screen, select **Begin Day 1**.

The answers are used to shape your local leadership rhythm. They do not create employee profiles or ask for employee personal information.

### Returning to the app

After setup, the app opens directly to **Today**. Your records are loaded from local browser storage. If the browser has been cleared or you are using a new device, restore a JSON backup before continuing.

## Navigation

The main workspace areas are:

- **Today** — your current operating view.
- **Journey** — your First 90 Days milestones.
- **Huddle** — the current alignment foundation screen.
- **Work** — focused commitments, risks, decisions, follow-ups, and actions.
- **Review** — close the day and prepare tomorrow.

Improve and Playbook are supporting areas available from their workflow links and routes. Settings is available from the gear icon on desktop and mobile.

On smaller screens, use the bottom navigation. On wider screens, use the sidebar. The current area is identified with the active navigation state.

## Today

Today is the place to start the workday.

### Daily focus

TalentisOS generates a short local focus statement from the information in your plan. It may point you toward a risk, a decision, your first priority, or choosing an outcome when the plan is empty.

### Recurring meetings

Today shows the nearest upcoming recurring meeting and a countdown such as **In 3 days** or **Tomorrow**. Select **Set up meetings** or **Manage meetings** to add a meeting name, cadence, time, and next meeting date.

Supported cadences are weekly, fortnightly, and monthly. Schedules remain on the current device. When a scheduled date has passed, TalentisOS advances the next occurrence locally according to the selected cadence.

### First 90 Days prompt

Today shows the next Journey milestone and a link to continue the First 90 Days path.

### Priorities

Today supports up to three priorities. A priority should describe an outcome, not only an activity.

To add one:

1. Select **Add a priority**.
2. Enter the outcome.
3. Add why it matters.
4. Add a due point.
5. Save it.

You can edit, reorder, complete, and reopen priorities. Once three priorities exist, the app prevents adding a fourth until one is removed or changed.

### Signals and meetings

Today brings together:

- Carryover from previous work
- Critical risks
- Decisions due or overdue
- Overdue follow-ups
- Meetings recorded in the daily plan

These sections are deliberately compact. They help you decide what needs attention without becoming a large analytics dashboard.

### Leadership prompt and end-of-day status

The leadership prompt provides a short local reminder such as defining the outcome or communicating risk early. The end-of-day status shows whether the current day has been reviewed.

## Journey: First 90 Days

Journey provides a progressive path for starting or strengthening your leadership practice. It does not create employee records or a complex project plan.

### Stages

The Journey contains four stages:

1. **First 7 Days** — listen, observe, establish clarity, meet the team, and choose the first focus.
2. **First 30 Days** — understand the operation and establish a reliable leadership rhythm.
3. **Days 31–60** — strengthen execution, accountability, handovers, and blocker removal.
4. **Days 61–90** — build sustainable standards, decisions, improvements, and the next leadership focus.

Each stage contains practical milestones with a short summary and checklist.

### Working with milestones

The Journey shows:

- Your current stage
- Completed milestone count
- Overall progress
- The current milestone
- The next milestones
- The checklist for the selected milestone

Select a next milestone card to display what it entails. The detail panel shows its purpose and checklist.

From the detail panel you can:

- Select **Mark milestone complete**.
- Select **Print milestone** to print the displayed milestone.

When a milestone is completed, TalentisOS records its completion date locally.

To review completed work:

1. Open **Review completed steps**.
2. Read the completed milestone summary and checklist.
3. Use the checked circle beside a milestone to mark it incomplete.

When you uncheck a completed milestone, it is removed from completed progress and returned to the active Journey.

### Swipe navigation

On a touch device, swipe left or right in the Journey to browse milestones. Buttons and cards remain available, so swiping is optional. Users who prefer reduced motion still receive the same information and controls without decorative animation.

### Prepare to Meet Your Team

The first recommended milestone is **Prepare to Meet Your Team**. Select **Open meeting builder** to prepare:

- Purpose
- Introduction
- Questions to ask
- Expectations to share
- How to close the conversation

Select **Save meeting plan**. The plan is stored locally and can be revisited while working through the milestone.

## Huddle

Huddle is currently a foundation screen for the morning alignment experience. It explains the intended purpose of bringing priorities, decisions, risks, and discussion into one clear moment.

The full interactive huddle flow is not currently presented as a completed feature. Use Today and Work for the current priority, risk, decision, and follow-up records.

## L10 Meeting

Open **Review → L10 meeting** for the weekly local leadership meeting. Set and save the **Week ending** date for the current meeting. The meeting uses a fixed 90-minute agenda:

1. Segue — leadership and business/customer/community bests
2. Scorecard — weekly goals, actuals, and on/off-track status
3. Rock review — outcome commitments and progress
4. Headlines — customer, team-area, or operating updates
5. To-Dos — review, complete, and carry forward actions
6. IDS — Identify, Discuss, Solve the highest-priority Issues
7. Conclude — actions, cascading messages, rating, and improvement

### L10 setup

Select **Meeting setup** to configure the meeting day, start time, team areas, facilitator area, scribe area, and rating target. The duration is fixed at 90 minutes.

### Scorecard

Scorecard metrics are entered manually. Each metric has a name, team area, direction (**At least** or **At most**), and weekly goal. During the meeting, enter the actual number. TalentisOS calculates whether it is On track or Off track. The traffic-light indicator shows green for On track, amber for Not entered, and red for Off track, with text labels included for accessibility. Use **Edit** to change a metric or **Delete** to remove it. An off-track metric can be added to Issues.

### Rocks

Rocks are quarterly-style leadership outcomes. Add the outcome, team area, and due date. Review the status during the Rock review and add an off-track Rock to Issues when it needs attention.

### Headlines and To-Dos

Headlines can describe customer/community, team-area, or operating news. Do not enter employee names or personal records. To-Dos store a title, team area, due date, and Done/Not done status. Incomplete To-Dos remain available for the next meeting.

### IDS and Issues

Add an Issue manually or promote an off-track Scorecard metric or Rock. Open an Issue to record:

- Identify
- Discuss
- Solve

The solved outcome can create a TalentisOS decision, follow-up, improvement, or cascading message. Mark the Issue solved or carry it forward.

### Timer and history

The active agenda section shows its duration and a non-blocking timer. Start, pause, and resume the timer; its state is stored locally. Previous L10 meetings are available in the collapsed history section.

## Work

Work is a focused operating list, not a project-management board.

### Work groups

Work items are grouped into:

- **Now** — active work that needs attention now.
- **Next** — work to pick up after the current focus.
- **Later** — useful work that is not yet immediate.
- **Waiting** — work waiting on another action, decision, or condition.

The Now group is limited to three active leadership actions.

### Work types

Work supports:

- **Priority** — an outcome that matters.
- **Risk** — something that could affect an outcome, customer, team, or deadline.
- **Decision** — a choice or trade-off that work is waiting on.
- **Follow-up** — a commitment that needs checking or closing.
- **Action** — a focused leadership action.

### Add and edit work

Use **Add work item** or a quick-add action to create a record. The fields change according to the selected type.

For a risk, record the impact, risk level, immediate action, and escalation information where relevant.

For a decision, record the decision required, options or context, due point, and review information.

For a follow-up, record what needs to happen, due date, status, and waiting or completion information.

You can edit records, move them between groups, complete them, delete them, and use **Undo** after a deletion or completion where available. Work forms autosave drafts locally while you are editing.

### Useful work views

Filters help you see follow-ups that are due today, upcoming, waiting, overdue, or complete. Today automatically surfaces critical risks, decisions due, and overdue follow-ups.

## Review

Review closes the current day and prepares the next one.

### End-of-day review

Review helps you consider:

- What was completed
- What remains incomplete
- What risks or blockers remain
- What decisions are waiting
- What should carry forward
- What should improve
- What tomorrow needs first

The review reuses records already entered in Today and Work. It is not a separate reporting system.

### Finish the day

1. Open **Review**.
2. Check the suggested completed and open items.
3. Confirm carryover, risks, decisions, and improvement notes.
4. Review tomorrow’s suggested items.
5. Reorder, remove, or add a tomorrow item as needed.
6. Select **Finish Day** or the review completion action.

Finishing the day stores a local closure snapshot and prepares the tomorrow plan.

### Tomorrow preparation

Tomorrow’s plan can include:

- Items to carry forward
- Meetings
- Risks
- Decisions
- Follow-ups

Confirm the plan when it is ready. The next day’s Today view can then begin with the prepared context.

### Daily history

Completed days are available in history. History is read-only by default. Use the explicit edit action when you need to correct a historical day.

## Weekly Review

Open the weekly review from Review. It uses existing local records from the selected week.

### Reflection questions

The review guides you through:

1. What was achieved?
2. What remained incomplete?
3. What risks repeated?
4. What decisions were delayed?
5. What consumed unnecessary time?
6. What should continue?
7. What should stop?
8. What should improve?
9. What are next week’s three priorities?

### Automatic local summary

TalentisOS calculates a simple summary without a server or complex charts, including:

- Priorities completed
- Priorities carried forward
- Repeated risks
- Overdue follow-ups
- Decisions completed
- Improvements captured
- Most common blocker
- Morning preparations completed
- Huddles completed
- End-of-day reviews completed

### Weekly output

Save the review with:

- Three priorities for next week
- One operating improvement
- One leadership focus

Repeated risks can be converted directly into an improvement so the review leads to a practical next step.

## Improve

Improve is for small, actionable operating changes. It is not a Lean certification system, complex project planner, or benefits dashboard.

### Capture an improvement

Record:

- What is not working?
- What should change?
- Why would it help?
- What is the next step?

Choose one category:

- Simplify
- Remove delay
- Improve clarity
- Reduce error
- Improve handover
- Improve meeting
- Improve customer outcome
- Improve workflow

Choose one status:

- Captured
- Reviewing
- Testing
- Implemented
- Closed

Improvements can be edited, completed, or deleted. The best improvement is small enough to test and clear enough to act on.

## Playbook

Playbook provides concise local guidance at the moment it is needed. It does not require internet access and is not a learning-management system.

### Find guidance

Use local search to search topic titles, groups, and guidance. Topic groups help narrow the list. You can also browse:

- Saved topics
- Recently viewed topics
- All topic groups

### Topic structure

Every guidance topic is organised as:

- **When to use it**
- **What to do**
- **What to say**
- **What to avoid**
- **What success looks like**

Topics include preparing the day, setting priorities, delegating, following up, managing risk, escalating early, making decisions, removing blockers, leading meetings, handling competing priorities, closing the day, running a weekly review, capturing improvements, and starting the first 90 days.

Open a topic to save it locally or remove it from saved topics. Contextual guidance links can open a relevant topic from a workflow without requiring you to leave the task permanently.

## Settings and appearance

Open Settings from the gear icon. You can choose:

- Light appearance
- System appearance
- Dark appearance

The interface supports responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts, safe-area insets, visible focus states, and reduced-motion preferences.

## Data management

Open **Settings → Data** for backup, restore, import, export, snapshots, and deletion.

### JSON backup

Select **Export backup** to download a complete local workspace backup. It includes settings, daily plans, priorities, huddles, work items, risks, decisions, follow-ups, daily reviews, tomorrow plans, weekly reviews, improvements, saved Playbook topics, onboarding state, and Journey state.

Backup filenames use a date, for example:

`TalentisOS_Backup_2026-08-01.json`

Keep the file somewhere safe if the data matters.

### Restore a JSON backup

1. Open **Settings → Data**.
2. Select **Restore backup**.
3. Choose a JSON file.
4. Review the file type, format, export date, and record counts.
5. Choose either:
   - **Replace current data** — clears the current workspace after creating an automatic pre-restore backup.
   - **Merge into current data** — adds the imported records to the current workspace.
6. Select **Restore backup** or cancel.

Invalid, unsupported, or malformed files are rejected before existing workspace data is changed.

### CSV export

CSV export is available for:

- Priorities
- Risks
- Decisions
- Follow-ups
- Improvements
- Daily summaries
- Weekly summaries

CSV files can be opened in spreadsheet software. Filenames use the dataset and date, for example:

`TalentisOS_Risks_2026-08-01.csv`

### CSV import

CSV import is available for priorities, risks, decisions, follow-ups, and improvements.

1. Choose the dataset.
2. Select **Download template** if you need the correct columns.
3. Choose **Import CSV**.
4. Review the row count and validation messages.
5. Correct any row-level errors before importing.
6. Choose how to handle duplicate IDs:
   - Skip existing records
   - Replace existing records
7. Select **Import rows**.

Date, column, and required-field errors are reported before records are written.

### Local snapshots

TalentisOS creates rotating local snapshots:

- Up to seven daily snapshots
- Up to four weekly snapshots

Use **Restore** beside a snapshot to preview and restore it. Snapshots stay on the current device.

### Delete all data

Delete all data is destructive. Before using it:

1. Export a backup.
2. Read the warning.
3. Enter the requested confirmation phrase.
4. Confirm deletion.

The application does not delete data silently from a normal workflow.

## Offline and PWA use

### Install on iPad or iPhone

1. Open <https://n365mr.github.io/TalentisOS/> in Safari while online.
2. Wait for the app to load completely.
3. Tap **Share**.
4. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Confirm the name **TalentisOS**.
6. Tap **Add**.
7. Open TalentisOS from the Home Screen.
8. Complete onboarding.
9. Create a test priority or work item.
10. Close and reopen the app.
11. Enable Airplane Mode.
12. Confirm the app opens and the test data remains available.
13. Disable Airplane Mode.

### Offline behavior

After the first successful online load, the app shell and previously loaded same-origin assets are cached locally. Core records remain in IndexedDB. The service worker provides an offline navigation fallback and cleans up older cache versions.

Service workers require HTTPS or a local development server. They do not run when `index.html` is opened directly with `file://`. Direct file mode still provides a local application bundle, but it does not provide service-worker caching.

### Updates

When a new application version is available, TalentisOS shows a calm notification. Choose:

- **Update now** — activates the waiting service worker and reloads after confirmation.
- **Later** — keeps the current version running.

Save or finish important drafts before choosing Update now. Saved IndexedDB records are preserved.

### iPad layout and Files support

The app is designed to work in portrait, landscape, Split View, and Stage Manager where supported by the device. Use the browser file picker or native share/download interface for JSON and CSV files. Exported files can be saved to the iPad Files app.

## Accessibility and comfortable use

TalentisOS is designed for:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen-reader labels and semantic landmarks
- Logical heading order
- Visible focus indicators
- Dialog close controls and Escape handling
- Touch targets suitable for iPad and mobile
- Text labels that do not rely on colour alone
- Dark mode and contrast-aware surfaces
- Reduced-motion preferences
- Responsive text and layout behavior

If animations are uncomfortable, enable reduced motion in the operating system or browser. The Journey remains fully usable through cards and buttons without swipe gestures.

## Troubleshooting

### The app does not load

- Confirm the GitHub Pages URL is exactly <https://n365mr.github.io/TalentisOS/>.
- Reload once while online.
- If using the installed PWA, open the URL in Safari to confirm the latest shell is available.
- If using direct `file://` mode, remember that service workers are unavailable there.

### My data appears to be missing

- Confirm you are using the same browser and device profile.
- Check whether private browsing or browser site-data settings changed.
- Do not clear site data before exporting a backup.
- Use Settings → Data → Restore backup if you have a JSON export.

### Restore fails

- Confirm the file is a TalentisOS JSON backup.
- Re-export the original backup if possible.
- Check the preview error and record counts.
- Unsupported versions, invalid dates, missing collections, and malformed JSON are rejected.

### CSV import reports errors

- Download the template for the selected dataset.
- Keep the required column names unchanged.
- Correct date values and required fields.
- Remove or fix invalid rows, then import again.
- Use duplicate handling deliberately: skip existing records or replace them.

### Offline mode is unavailable

- Load the deployed HTTPS app successfully once while online.
- Do not test service-worker behavior from `file://`.
- Check that browser storage and service workers are enabled.
- Reopen the app online and wait for the shell to finish loading before testing Airplane Mode.

### An update notification appears delayed

Service-worker updates depend on browser caching and lifecycle timing. Keep using the current version or select **Update now** when convenient. Important saved records remain in local storage.

### I cleared browser data

Clearing browser or website data can remove the local workspace. Restore the latest JSON backup through Settings → Data. Without an export or local snapshot, cleared browser data may not be recoverable.

## End of Day

End of Day is a guided daily close-out assistant available from the primary navigation. It moves through Review Today, Outstanding Work, Risks, Tomorrow, and Close Day. It uses shared local work-item records, so tasks created in EOD are available in Work as well.

The dashboard shows Outstanding, Risks, Tomorrow’s Priorities, and Completed Today. Open work can be completed, new tasks and risks can be captured, and up to three open tasks can be selected for tomorrow. A handover note can be added before closing the day. EOD progress is saved locally and can be resumed after leaving or reloading the app. Closed EOD records appear in EOD History and are included in JSON backups.

Tasks support optional subtasks in their detail view. Subtask progress is shown as a simple percentage bar and updates from the local records.

## Product boundaries

TalentisOS intentionally does not include:

- Employee profiles or employee personal records
- HR records or performance reviews
- Payroll or recruitment
- Skills matrices
- Surveillance or time tracking
- CRM or ERP functionality
- Chat or email
- A full project-management platform
- Complex project plans or improvement dashboards
- Financial-benefit calculations
- Lean certification workflows
- A cloud account or automatic synchronization

TalentisOS is a practical leadership rhythm tool. Keep records focused on outcomes, risks, decisions, commitments, reviews, and improvements rather than personal or sensitive information.
